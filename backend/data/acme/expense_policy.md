# Acme Corp Expense Policy

## Submitting Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted in the Expense Portal within 30 days of purchase. Every expense needs an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted after 30 days require written approval from a director.

## Travel
Flights must be booked through the Acme Travel Desk in economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than eight hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 200 dollars per night in most cities and up to 300 dollars per night in high-cost cities listed in the Travel Guide.

## Meals
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day. Client dinners are reimbursed up to 100 dollars per person and require the names of all attendees. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless a director pre-approves it.

## Home Office
Remote employees can claim a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars for a desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts are required.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll.
