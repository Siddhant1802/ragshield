# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month, starting from the employee's first day. Part-time employees receive PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carry-Over
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused days above 5 expire on December 31. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year.

## Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted in the HR Portal. Absences longer than three consecutive working days must be requested at least two weeks in advance. Managers should approve or decline requests within five working days.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note is required for sick leave longer than three consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## Public Holidays
Acme observes 11 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR Portal every December for the following year.
