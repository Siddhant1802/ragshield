# Acme Corp IT Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days. Employees may not reuse any of their last 10 passwords. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. Acme IT will never ask for your password by email, chat, or phone.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the Acme Authenticator app. MFA is required for email, the HR Portal, and the VPN.

## Resetting Your Password
To reset your password, go to the official self-service page at https://password.acme.example. If you cannot access it, contact the IT Service Desk in person or call the number on the back of your employee badge.

## VPN
Employees working outside the office must connect through the Acme VPN. Install the VPN client from the Acme Software Center only. Do not use personal or third-party VPN services for company work.

## Devices
Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours. Personal USB storage devices are not allowed on company laptops.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button in your mail client. Never enter your credentials on a page you reached from an email link.
